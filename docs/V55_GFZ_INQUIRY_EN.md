# GFZ consultation draft · unsent

**Subject — Historical observed F10.7 provenance and missing records on 9 May and 16 June 2021**

Dear GFZ Kp and solar-index team,

I am preparing a retrospective TIE-GCM hindcast that uses GFZ daily observed F10.7 in sfu. The GFZ public API returns a missing value (−1) for both 2021-05-09 and 2021-06-16. Could you clarify the measurement source, nominal observation time, quality acceptance rules, and publication or revision history for these days?

For each date, is there a traceable original observed measurement or a published correction consistent with the existing GFZ Fobs definition? If so, could you share the official record identifier or link, timestamp, quality flag and revision date? The study requires the observed value. The adjusted series is tracked separately.

If the measurement was unavailable or rejected, could you point to the applicable archive or quality note and advise whether GFZ recommends retaining the gap or has a published convention for retrospective studies? We will preserve the original missing flags while reviewing the evidence.

Thank you for your guidance.
