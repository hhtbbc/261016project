# ESA Swarm archive consultation draft · unsent

**Subject — Historical Swarm-A MOD 0504 inputs and delivery evidence for DNSAPOD 0301**

Dear ESA Swarm archive and product support team,

I am documenting the provenance and coordinate reference of two Swarm A DNSAPOD products for a retrospective model comparison. The public catalogue agrees with the identifiers and byte lengths of the files held by the project:

- `SW_OPER_DNSAPOD_2__20210621T000000_20210621T235930_0301.ZIP` (140,229 bytes)
- `SW_OPER_DNSAPOD_2__20220621T000000_20220621T235930_0301.ZIP` (138,722 bytes)

The original HDR files cite these inputs:

- `SW_OPER_MODA_SC_1B_20210621T000000_20210621T235959_0504.sp3`
- `SW_OPER_MODA_SC_1B_20220621T000000_20220621T235959_0504.sp3`

The current public listings show baseline 0601 for these dates. Is baseline 0504 available through an authorized historical archive? Could you provide the corresponding MODA 0504 definitions for the terrestrial frame realization and reference epoch, SP3 clock and sampling, satellite reference point, and any correction needed to compare with DNSAPOD geodetic coordinates? We would also appreciate the applicable 0301 revision of the Level 2 specification referenced as SW-DS-DTU-GS-0001, including the reference ellipsoid, altitude definition and quality flags.

Could you point us to a publisher checksum, a signed release record or another reproducible official delivery record for the two exact 0301 ZIP products? We will keep the 0504 and 0601 inputs distinct.

Thank you for your assistance.
